The scripts in this repository (`AC1Calculator.py` and `RowStatistics.py`) were programmed to obtain Gwet's AC1 coefficient and expert agreement per-metric in a reliable way. It is important to mention that, to calculate the AC1 coefficient, we leveraged the [irrCAC package](https://irrcac.readthedocs.io/en/latest/usage/usage_raw_data.html#Gwet's-Coefficient).

## Results

As we discussed in our paper (reference pending), we calculated that (1) on average, expert agreement per-metric was **82%**, and (2) inter-rater reliability was _substantial_ with an AC1 value of **0.67**. More details regarding these statistics can be found in our article.
